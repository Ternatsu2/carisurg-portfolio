Week 6 update. I started with logistic regression and a shallow decision tree. The first logistic model found four of sixteen ESI 1 visits. After the interim feedback, I tuned class weights on a validation split inside the training data and left the final test set untouched. The tuned logistic model found seven of sixteen. It produced twenty-three false ESI 1 alerts, compared with four before tuning, while overall accuracy stayed near sixty-eight percent. Five caught cases had a stroke-alert flag. The misses were more mixed, including shortness of breath, hypotension and altered mental status. That is a useful direction, not a deployment result. I would keep clinician review and test the trade-off with more ESI 1 data before using it in triage.

Risk method

Initial and residual risk = likelihood (1 rare to 5 almost certain) x severity (1 negligible to 5 catastrophic). Controls are layered because each one can fail.

1. Clinical boundary

No autonomous ESI, diagnosis, treatment or model-to-EHR write.

2. Input protection

Provenance, time, validation, read-back and correction.

3. Human confirmation

Nurse confirm/override; patient consent, erase, decline or call nurse.

4. Technical fallback

Fail closed, no stale suggestion, manual path and device lockout.

5. Monitoring

Audit events, class and subgroup review, pause triggers and sign-off.

Accessibility and inclusion

Number and text repeat every colour cue. HCI supports keyboard use and AA contrast. HRI supports captions, touch, push-to-talk, adjustable screen and volume, language choice, and immediate nurse or interpreter handoff.

Residual-risk decision

No control removes under-triage, rushed confirmation, overheard speech or device failure. The first deployment remains a limited shadow-mode pilot. Clinical governance, Clinical IT, infection prevention, biomedical engineering and patient experience must accept their assigned residual risks and can pause the pilot independently.
